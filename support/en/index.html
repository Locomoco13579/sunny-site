FAQ

No ad is shown

Check your connection and try again after a moment. Depending on region and time, an ad may not be available.

Stamina is not restoring

Stamina restores over time, and watching an ad can restore it too. Set your device date and time to automatic.

Moving my save data

Save data lives only on your device and there is no cloud sync. Deleting the App removes it.

Changing ad consent or tracking

Consent: "Ad consent settings" in the App (shown where required). Tracking: iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See the Privacy Policy.

I saw an inappropriate ad

Use "Report ad" on the ad screen to email us.
