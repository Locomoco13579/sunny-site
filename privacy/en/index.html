ONE LOOP Inc. ("we") explains below how information is handled in the game "Sunny" (the "App").

1. Summary

  • There are no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your save data stays on your device only. It is not sent to us (we do not run a server for the App).
  • The App shows Google AdMob rewarded ads. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect some information (see section 3).
  • You can play the whole game even if you do not allow tracking (ATT).

2. Information stored on your device (not sent to us)

  • Save data (progress, settings, stamina, etc.). Stored on your device only and removed when you delete the App. There is no cloud sync.
  • Anonymous on-device play records (such as episodes played). Never sent off the device. Off by default in the release version.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) provided by Google LLC. When an ad is shown, Google and its advertising partners may collect the following information, which is handled under Google's privacy policy.

InformationMain purposes
Approximate locationAd delivery, analytics, app functionality
Device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, IDFA)Ad delivery and measurement, analytics (may be used for tracking)
Product interaction (ad impressions, taps, etc.)Ad delivery, analytics, app functionality
Advertising dataAd delivery, analytics
Performance, diagnostic and crash dataAnalytics, finding problems
  • We do not collect this information on our own servers and we do not sell it.
  • Watching an ad restores stamina. Ads are optional.
  • See Google: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

  • The first time you try to watch an ad, iOS shows a one-time tracking permission request.
  • If you allow it, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is used for ads that suit you and for ad measurement.
  • If you decline or do not answer, non-personalized ads are shown without the advertising identifier. The game and its rewards are the same either way.
  • To change it later: iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

5. Consent (GDPR and similar laws)

  • In the EEA, the UK and other regions that require consent, Google's consent tool (UMP) shows a consent screen before ads.
  • You can change or withdraw consent at any time from "Ad consent settings" in the App (shown in regions where it is required).
  • In regions that do not require it (such as Japan), no consent screen is shown.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device is removed when you delete the App.
  • For ad-related information held by Google, use Google's My Ad Center or reset your advertising identifier in iOS.
  • Because we do not hold personal information about players through the App, there is normally nothing for us to delete. If you have questions, contact us below.

7. Reporting inappropriate ads

You can report a problem ad by email from "Report ad" in the App. The email pre-fills only technical information (app version, device model, OS, ad placement) and no personal information. If you send it, we receive your email address and message and use them only to respond.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. It contains horror themes. See the App Store age rating.

9. Sharing with third parties

We do not provide your information to third parties except as required by law. The information Google collects for ads is described in section 3.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and its date, and tell you in the App for important changes.
